About

I'm a Computer Science student from Awaran, Balochistan, finishing my degree at Balochistan University of Engineering & Technology, Khuzdar as class topper, with a CGPA of 3.94 out of 4.00.

My interests sit on one path: from data science into artificial intelligence, machine learning and computer vision. My final year project, CARE-Safe, is where these come together: a real-time face recognition system that verifies who is collecting a child from school.

Alongside coursework I completed a full-stack development internship at CodeAlpha, built web and mobile apps, led volunteers at my university's Student Developer Society, and taught part-time at a school in Hub Chowki. I keep learning through courses from Google, Cisco and Saylor, and next I plan to pursue a Master's in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.
